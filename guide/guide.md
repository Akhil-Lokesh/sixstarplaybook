## Characters and story {#characters-and-story}

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are a criminal couple whose easy score goes wrong, pulling them into a conspiracy that spans Leonida ([Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI), [Xbox](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/grand-theft-auto-vi)). Their dynamic is modeled on Bonnie and Clyde, and Lucia is the series' first main female lead you can't skip ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_6)).

| Character | Who they are |
| --- | --- |
| Jason Duval | Former soldier who grew up among grifters; works for drug runners in the Keys and lives rent-free with Brian in exchange for help on shakedowns |
| Lucia Caminos | Raised to fight, just out of Leonida Penitentiary; wants the good life her mom hoped for after leaving Liberty City |
| Cal Hampton | Jason's friend; a stay-at-home conspiracy thinker who listens in on Coast Guard radio |

| Raul Bautista | Veteran bank robber who recruits crews for risky, big-money jobs |

All character details are from Rockstar's [Only in Leonida](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida) page. It is the first main GTA since 1997 not written by Dan Houser, who left in 2020 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_6)).

## Gameplay systems {#gameplay-systems}

The biggest changes are a six-star wanted level where cops must actually see you, a two-person crew you can split up, and a Criminal Profile that tracks how you commit crimes. Items marked (preview) come from press hands-on reports in August 2026, not from Rockstar directly.

### Wanted level and police

- Wanted level now goes up to six stars, one more than GTA 5's cap of five ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-wanted-system-goes-up-to-6-stars-and-lets-police-track-your-face-your-clothes-and-your-car/), Rob Nelson to IGN, Aug 27, 2026).
- A crime is only reported if someone sees it or an alarm trips.
- Four icons show what police know: your face, your clothes, your car, and whether the couple is together.
- Stars turn red when cops lose contact and switch to searching; the map shows a search circle instead of each police car ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/everything-shown-at-the-grand-theft-auto-6-netflix-special/)).
- Ways out: swap to a car they don't know, change masks, hats or clothes, change your hair, or split Jason and Lucia up (preview, [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-new-details-netflix-extended-look-previews/)).
- Nelson's tip: at three stars, leave the car and run through alleys unless you have a very fast car or bike.
- Six stars means an all-out manhunt (preview).

### Jason and Lucia together

- Switch between them in the open world, call the other one over, and swap driving and shooting roles in a getaway (preview).
- Missions can be played together or apart; in robberies one holds up the store while the other drives ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/everything-shown-at-the-grand-theft-auto-6-netflix-special/)).
- Their bond rises with shared activities and drops if you ignore texts; romance is optional and can be played platonically (preview; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_6)).

### Criminal Profile

- A reputation system in place of Red Dead Redemption 2's Honor. It tracks whether you keep crimes "professional" or cause needless violence and destruction; mission violence doesn't count. It changes how people react and may affect the story (preview; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_6)).

### Heists, money and car theft

- Cash comes from store robberies, bank heists and stealing and selling cars; some story steps need a set amount of money (preview).
- Safes: blow them fast and risk damaging the loot, or find the combination for full value (preview).
- Cars come in security tiers: a slim jim for old cars, key cloners for luxury ones; some have GPS trackers that alert police. Stolen goods go to fences (preview).
- Vehicles shown include fan boats, kayaks, dirt bikes and helicopters; each lead can keep a personal vehicle, with a trunk for spare weapons and an outfit ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/everything-shown-at-the-grand-theft-auto-6-netflix-special/); preview).

### Combat

- You can wound instead of kill, by shooting an arm, a leg or a weapon out of a hand; flashy kills can trigger slow motion (preview).

### Phone and in-game social media

- The phone is a physical object in the character's hand (preview).
- Snapmatic is the social media parody; you can browse it but not post, since you're fugitives (preview).
- Other apps: WAINK scans a car's value, locks and alarm before you steal it; Ryde Me is a rideshare app (preview).
- Influencers livestream around you, with chat on screen ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/everything-shown-at-the-grand-theft-auto-6-netflix-special/)).

### Activities and world

- Pool, mini golf, kayaking, scuba diving, parachuting and skydiving, workouts, the zoo, dirt bike races, basketball and wrestling ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_6); preview).
- Your body changes with diet and exercise, and days without sleep show on your face ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_6)).
- Wildlife can be studied and logged on a checklist; you can pet dogs (preview).
- Barbers, salons and clothing stores let you change your look ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_6)).

## Map, online and technical details {#map-online-and-technical-details}

The game launches single-player only, at 30fps on both consoles, on a map Rockstar calls "very big" without giving a size.

### Map size

- Rockstar has given no official measurement ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-new-details-netflix-extended-look-previews/)).
- Rob Nelson reportedly compared it to about three times Red Dead Redemption 2, passed on by YouTuber TGG after a studio visit ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/grand-theft-auto-6s-map-is-three-times-bigger-than-red-dead-redemption-2/), Aug 28, 2026). Other preview estimates say about twice GTA 5's playable area.

### GTA Online

- There is no online multiplayer at launch; Rockstar calls GTA 6 a single-player experience ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/psa-gta-6-does-not-have-online-multiplayer-at-launch), Nov 13, 2026; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_6)).
- Bloomberg reports online is expected in 2027, and Twitch's CEO said multiplayer comes "next year." Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed a date ([Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/gaming/gta-vis-online-mode-could-arrive-in-2027-well-after-the-single-player-campaign-begins/), Oct 14, 2026).
